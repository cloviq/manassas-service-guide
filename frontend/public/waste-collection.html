01

Start here

Who collects your waste?

These rules are for residents within the City of Manassas using City collection.

Check the jurisdiction, not just the mailing address.

A Manassas mailing address or ZIP code does not confirm City collection eligibility. Confirm the provider for your home before following this guide.

City of Manassas, Manassas Park, and Prince William County are different jurisdictions. If your building, landlord, or HOA arranges service, confirm your collection arrangement with them.

Official guidanceCity service area and eligibility

Remember: A map feature or nearby collection area is not proof that your home is served.

02

Plan the handoff

Find the day. Then prepare.

  1. Open the City’s Waste Wise tool. Use the widget on the City website to look up your collection schedule. Check current holiday changes there too.
  2. Have carts out before 6 a.m. The City’s trash page says to place carts out before 6 a.m. on collection day and bring them back after collection.
  3. Keep loose trash off the street. Do not place bags or raw trash directly on the street. Check preparation instructions for your collection stream.
Find Waste Wise on the City website ↗

Official guidanceTrash preparation and collection instructions

Remember: Yard waste, regular trash, and bulk items can have different collection days. This guide does not calculate your personal pickup day.

03

Sort before setout

The same material can need a different route.

Curbside recycling

Clean containers & paper

The City lists food and beverage metal cans and plastic containers, paper, and flattened cardboard. Rinse food containers. Do not bag recycling in plastic.

Separate glass drop-off

Glass bottles & jars

Keep them out of the curbside recycling cart. Take clean bottles and jars to the purple glass-only container outside 8500 Public Works Drive, available 24/7.

04

Two different services

Bagged yard waste is not the leaf route.

Regular yard waste

Monday collection

The City says to set yard waste out Sunday night for Monday collection. Use paper yard-waste bags or private containers marked “Yard Waste.” No plastic bags.

Tie brush into bundles no longer than 4 feet, with branches no more than 3 inches in diameter and bundles no heavier than 50 pounds.

Yard waste preparation and current limits
Seasonal loose leaves

A separate vacuum route

The City’s fall leaf page describes an October–January service and displays a 2026–27 schedule. Check your assigned collection period before raking leaves to the street or sidewalk edge.

Do not treat Monday yard-waste pickup as your loose-leaf collection date.

Current fall leaf guidance and schedule

The yard-waste page’s current Fall guidance allows up to 10 bags and 10 brush bundles per pickup. Do not assume seasonal limits apply year-round; check the current page and holiday schedule.

Map limits: Older leaf-layer collection windows in this prototype do not state a year. They are not a live schedule or an eligibility determination. Use the current City leaf page, which links its official lookup.

05

Make arrangements first

Large item? Do not just leave it out.

48 hoursminimum advance scheduling
3 itemsmaximum per scheduled pickup

Call the City Trashline at 703-257-8252 at least 48 hours before your bulk collection day. The dedicated bulk page allows up to 3 items per household per scheduled pickup.

The page lists Wednesday for single-family homes and Tuesday or Friday for townhouses. Your bulk day may differ from your regular trash day. Confirm the day and accepted items with the City before setting anything out.

TVs and computers need separate scheduling. Chemicals, tires, lawn mowers, and large construction debris are not regular bulk pickup items. Check the dedicated page for alternatives.

Official guidanceBulk rules, accepted items and scheduling instructions

Why we say “bulk day,” not “trash day”

The general trash page says to call 48 hours before trash day. The dedicated bulk page says 48 hours before bulk day and warns that the days may differ. This guide follows the more specific bulk page; confirm your day with the City. This is an editorial reading of the sources, not staff confirmation.

06

Use the right next step

Turn what you learned into action.

Check a collection day

Use Waste Wise on the City’s trash and recycling page. Address lookup happens on the official service, not in this guide.

Report a problem

The same page’s “Report a Problem” button covers missed collection, cart exchange, and bulk scheduling. You can also call the Trashline during regular office hours.

Open City schedules and reporting options ↗

Missed pickup? The City pages give different recovery timing for later reports. Report promptly and ask the City to confirm next steps. This guide does not promise same-day service or decide when a pickup is missed.

Reading this page or opening a City link does not submit a request to the City. The service finder’s separate demo intake creates prototype tickets only.
